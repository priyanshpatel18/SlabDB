# Refund Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026.

Slab does not sell a paid product on `https://slab.priyanshpatel.com`.

## What Slab charges

Slab does not take card payments, in-app purchases, or a subscription fee for profiles or repositories.

## Network fees

Create, delegate, upload, and CLI push can spend **SOL** and **Irys** fees on Solana devnet (or another cluster the site names). Those fees go to validators and to Irys. Slab cannot reverse or refund a confirmed blockchain or Irys payment.

If a transaction fails before confirmation, the network rules apply. Slab does not hold a float to refund failed txs.

## Devnet

Devnet SOL has no cash value. Do not expect a money refund for devnet activity.

## If this changes

If Slab later sells a paid feature, this page will describe the cooling-off period, how to request a refund, and what is not refundable. Until then, there is no Slab product fee to refund.
